FAQ

Questions before you start

How much does it cost?

It’s a subscription. After the quiz you see the price and terms, before you pay.

How do I cancel?

You can cancel any time. Confirm the exact steps on the web and in the app

Do I need a tech background?

No. Lessons use plain language and no-code tools. If you can use everyday web apps, you can follow from the first lesson.

How much time does it take?

About 15 minutes a day. You choose the pace in the quiz.

Which AI tools will I use?

Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Make, Zapier, n8n, Midjourney and Notion AI. More than 30 tools in total.

Do I need to pay for AI tools?

No. Claude has a free plan that is enough to start. Lessons point out where a paid plan helps.

Phone, tablet or computer?

Any of them. Use the app or the web version, and your progress syncs.

What is the certificate?

A certificate of completion for finishing a program. You can add it to your LinkedIn profile.
